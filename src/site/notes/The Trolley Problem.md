## Description

 I wanted a classic Cable Car, but I never keep spiced rum on hand. So I zeroed in on the two strongest flavors of the likes of Captain Morgan/ Sailor Jerry with what I did have around for tiki cocktails. Great results, figured I'd share for others who are in the same ethical predicament. Pull the lever and make the Trolley Problem. Cheers!
## Ingredients

50 ml // 1 2/3 oz gold rum 
30 ml // 1 oz dry curaçao 
20 ml // 2/3 oz lemon juice 
5 ml // 
1 tsp cinnamon syrup 
2 dash vanilla bitters 


## Method

Shake with ice. 
Double strain, serving up in a glass prepared with a cinnamon-sugar rim. 
Garnish with an orange twist.
