## Description


## Ingredients

-  50ml Pure Kodoko
-  25ml fresh lemon juice
-  15ml honey syrup (1:1 honey & water)
-  1 egg white (optional for froth)
-  Lemon twist (garnish)
## Method
**Step1:** Add 50 ml Kodoko Rakshi

**Step2:** 25 ml fresh lime juice

**Step3:** 15 ml honey syrup and ice in a shaker.

**Step4:** Strain in a coupe glass and garnish with a lemon twist.
