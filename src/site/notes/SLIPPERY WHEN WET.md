## Description


## Ingredients

2 Strawberries

.50 oz (15 ml) Honey

.75 oz (45 ml) Lemon Juice

1 heaping bar spoon of plain Greek yogurt

2 oz Gin
## Method

Muddle Honey, Lemon and Strawberries

add Yogurt and Gin

Shake over Ice

Serve in rocks glass w/crushed or pebbled ice

Garnish w/cracked pepper & strawberries slices
