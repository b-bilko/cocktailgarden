## Description


## Ingredients

1.5 oz Blanco Tequila

.5 oz Elderflower Liqueur

.5 oz Blue Curacao

.75 oz Lime Juice

1 Egg White


## Method

Shake with ice, strain into a rocks glass, garnish with a dehydrated orange wheel, pretend this last week never happened.
