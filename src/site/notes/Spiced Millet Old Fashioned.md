## Description

A Finger Millet twist on the classic Old Fashioned

## Ingredients

-  50 ml Pure Kodoko Rakshi
-  5 ml Demerara syrup
-  2 dashes Angostura bitter
-  1 dash orange bitter
-  1 large block of ice cube
-  Orange peel & cinnamon stick (garnish)
## Method

**Step1:**  Add 50 ml Pure Kodoko Rakshi

**Step2:** Add 5 mi Demerara syrup

**Step3:** Add 2 dashes Angostura bitter

**Step4:**  Add 1 dash orange bitter

**Step5:** Add 1 large block onice cube

**Step6:** Garnish with orange peel and cinnamon stick
