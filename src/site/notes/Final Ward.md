## Description


## Ingredients
0.75oz Chartreuse Verte 
0.75oz bourbon (Four Roses) 
0.75oz Luxardo maraschino liqueur 
0.75oz lemon juice

## Method

Shaken with ice, and served in a Reidel Nick & Nora glass, garnished with a Luxardo cherry.
